## Problem

The Heritage Christian University case study examines campus temperature, lighting, occupancy, connectivity, and environmental conditions, including a touch-free handwashing design for the cafeteria.

## Contribution

Academic study, sensor-to-cloud architecture design, report development, and prototype planning.

## Features and scope

- Study of temperature, lighting, occupancy, connectivity, and environmental conditions
- Designed sensor-to-cloud architecture
- Planned data storage and visualization
- Designed touch-free sensor-operated cafeteria handwashing tap
