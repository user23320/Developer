Each case study covers the problem, contribution, features, technology areas, current status, and evidence limits. Its evidence index groups existing documents/public references as **Available**, conditional restricted review as **Private**, and unpublished candidate artifacts as **Planned**. The indexes and template are published; no demonstration package, screenshot collection, architecture package, completed academic report/presentation, application code sample, or test-results package is attached yet. Creating an index does not make those artifacts available.

Public references and historical observations are carried forward from the existing case studies; external pages and workflows were not retested for the 11 October 2026 inventory update. These documents do not independently establish authorship, feature completion, security, or measured outcomes. The SafeRide company link is a general contact reference, and Smart Campus remains an academic design.

Project-specific technology areas come from the case studies and their stated sources. The portfolio's general stack is not assigned wholesale to individual projects.

Credentials, customer data, private infrastructure, payment information, and protected business logic are excluded. Future artifacts and separately arranged private reviews must follow the [publication checks](../evidence/README.md#publication-checks).
