# DALEAM EDU

[Project index](README.md) · [Portfolio](../README.md)

**A school-management platform shaped by approximately ten years of private-school experience, connecting academic records with the business of running a school.**

## Current status

**Ongoing — active development and upgrades**

DALEAM EDU has a public website and continues to develop. This case study preserves the portfolio's ongoing status; it does not claim that every described module has been independently tested or fully deployed. Updated on 6 October 2026 using public product information and the developer's account.

## Problem and origin

The developer reports approximately ten years of experience working in a private-school environment. This provided direct exposure to manual report preparation, fee collection, and the practical responsibilities that extend beyond classroom assessment.

An earlier reporting-oriented system used in that environment did not meet the school's broader operational needs, in the developer's experience. DALEAM EDU was conceived to connect those needs in a more comprehensive platform. This is an account of the developer's experience, not a comparative evaluation of competing products.

A school's income can come from tuition, feeding, transport, extra classes, and other categories. Schools may also supply stationery and uniforms while managing expenses and organizing activities. Academic reporting alone does not provide management with a complete financial and operational picture.

## Contribution

Designed the system architecture, database structure, backend workflows, and interfaces. Applied firsthand school experience to requirements analysis, with the goal of connecting student records, academic reporting, category-based revenue, expense recording, and management oversight.

The project's breadth reflects observed school responsibilities. The portfolio does not claim superiority over other school-management products without comparative evidence.

## Design emphasis

**Academic and operational information together.** The aim is to let a school manage learner progress while understanding its collections, obligations, and spending.

**Access suited to each role.** Owners, management, teachers, finance staff, and parents need different views and permissions.

**Management visibility.** Category-based income and expense records are intended to help management understand school operations. No independently measured financial improvement is claimed.

**Requirements grounded in experience.** The developer's school experience informed the problem definition and product direction. It is not presented as a formal survey or quantified research study.

## Technology areas

The public About page identifies PHP and MySQL/MariaDB, with multi-tenant separation and role-based access. Other relevant areas include relational database design, backend workflows, responsive interfaces, reporting, and SMS integration.

The homepage advertises installable/hybrid access. That alone does not establish full offline functionality or synchronization reliability; the offline behaviour documented for Assist Shop Management is not assumed to apply to DALEAM EDU.
