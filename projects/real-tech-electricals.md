The developer reports that the current customer, technician, and administrative workflows are complete and operating, including bookings, notifications, job records, and payment-status visibility. Status updated on 6 October 2026 based on the developer's operational account.

## Problem

Customers need a way to find and book technicians online. Technicians and administrators need connected records and timely updates to coordinate requests, field work, and customer communication.

## Contribution

Designed and developed the service-operations platform and the workflows connecting customers, technicians, and business administration. Completed the current development scope, including customer bookings, technician responses, field-job recording, notifications, and dashboard status visibility.

Technician recruitment and geographic service coverage are company responsibilities, separate from the developer's completed implementation.

## Features and scope

- Online technician discovery and customer bookings
- Technician assignment and job/ETA tracking
- Phone and dashboard notifications for technicians
- Technician access to booking details and response workflows
- Staff, technician, and administrative portals
- Field-job and customer-service record entry
- Payment-status visibility on relevant dashboards
- Customer notifications relating to service activity

## Technology areas

Service-workflow design, customer and staff interfaces, tracking, notifications, and controlled portal access.
